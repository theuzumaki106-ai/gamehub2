HOW TO PLAY

SURVIVE & ESCAPE WITH THE IDOL

↑ / W / SPACE / SWIPE UP

JUMP over fallen logs, chasms, and floor spikes.

↓ / S / SWIPE DOWN

SLIDE under low stone arches and carved beams.

← / A / SWIPE LEFT

TURN LEFT at corners, or change to the left lane.

→ / D / SWIPE RIGHT

TURN RIGHT at corners, or change to the right lane.

POWER-UPS:

• MAGNET: Attracts all nearby coins automatically.

• SHIELD: Protects against 1 fatal obstacle impact.

• BOOST: Turbo dash forward with total invulnerability.

• 2X MULTIPLIER: Doubles coins and distance points.
